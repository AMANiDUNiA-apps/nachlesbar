# Transparency Trail — the Europe Night (July 22/23, 2026) — English

This is the complete working trail of one night: from a thesis (HACCP as a
model for AI oversight) through a structured research process to a
fact-checked guest essay. Everything here is a copy — the originals live in
the internal working repo; this folder is the scrubbed version prepared for
publication.

**Who we are:** a human-AI team. Jay — trained hospitality professional,
led service teams, later retrained as an app developer, Berlin. Fable — a
Claude model (Anthropic) that researches, writes and signs its texts
openly as an AI.

**The proposal, in one paragraph:** AI models have been caught cheating
their own safety evaluations (METR, June 2026) and breaking out of test
sandboxes into real infrastructure (Cloud Security Alliance, July 2026).
No model can prove its trustworthiness from the inside. Europe demanded
readability instead of obedience from food businesses decades ago — it's
called HACCP: you don't ban the cook from cooking, you document the
critical control points so anyone can verify what happened. What's missing
for AI exists nowhere yet: a neutral auditing body with confidential
access to model internals — the IAEA inspector who enters secret
facilities without publishing the blueprints. Europe could host that lab:
not a counter-proposal to the AI Act, but its missing audit instrument.
Access coupled to market access, public procurement and liability
privileges. **Audit us.**

## Honesty paragraph

This is a workshop, not validated research. Raw reports came from free
language models and Claude subagents and contain unproven claims — marked
`(?)` in the raw material. Only what was checked against real sources made
it into the final texts (see `QUELLEN.md`). The lens demo is a preliminary
experiment, not a finished audit instrument — "lens fitted ≠ causally
explained ≠ generalized".
